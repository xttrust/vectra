About Northwind

We build the back office our families never had.

Northwind started at a kitchen table in Tuam, with a shoebox of receipts and a roofer who would rather be on a roof. Today it runs the admin for more than 1,200 small businesses.

Our story

Built by people who did the paperwork

Aoife Kelly spent ten years running the office of her father's roofing business: answering the phone between jobs, chasing invoices on Sunday evenings and keeping the diary in a notebook that lived in the van.

Every tool she tried was built for an accountant or a call centre, not for a crew of four in a county with patchy signal. So in 2019 she sat down with Seán, a friend from school who wrote software, and started again from the job itself: a customer rings, someone goes out, the work gets done, the money comes in.

That is still the whole idea. Northwind does the boring parts quietly, works on the phone in your pocket, and stays out of the way of the actual work.

What we believe

Three rules we will not break

Plain and kind

No jargon, no dark patterns, no small print that bites. If a screen needs a manual, we redo the screen.

Your data is yours

Hosted in the EU, never sold, and exportable in one click. Leaving should be as easy as joining.

Built to last

We are profitable and independent, so we answer to customers, not investors. No surprise shutdowns.

Milestones

How we got here

Slowly, on purpose, and with our customers in the room.

  1. A notebook becomes an app

    2019

    The first version runs one roofing business in Tuam. It does bookings and nothing else.

  2. Invoices and card payments

    2021

    Fifty businesses sign up by word of mouth. Pay-now links cut the average wait for payment from 31 days to 9.

  3. The team grows to nine

    2023

    We open an office on the Dublin Road and start answering the phone ourselves.

  4. 1,000 businesses

    2025

    Northwind reaches Scotland and Wales, still with no sales team and no adverts.

  5. Online booking for everyone

    2026

    Customers can book a slot from any website, on every plan, including the free one.
