How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

Most people set it up on their first evening and get a booking that week.

  1. Set your hours and services

    Say when you work, how long each job takes and how far you travel. Northwind keeps a buffer between jobs for the drive.

  2. Share one link

    Put it on your website, your Google profile, your van and your email signature. It works on any phone.

  3. Bookings land in your calendar

    With the address, the notes and any photos. The customer gets a text the day before, so fewer people forget.

Questions

About online booking

Anything else? Ask us, a real person replies within a working day.

Does it cost extra?

No. Online booking is part of every plan, including Solo, which is free. Text reminders are included up to 100 a month on Solo and without a limit on paid plans.

Can I approve bookings first?

Yes. Switch on approval for any service and requests wait for you to accept them. The customer hears back by text either way.

What if someone needs to cancel?

Every confirmation has a link to cancel or move the booking, up to a cut-off you set. Deposits follow the rules you choose.

Will it work with my website?

Yes. Paste one line into WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or plain HTML, or simply link to your booking page.
