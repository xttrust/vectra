What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Some are needed for the site to work at all; others help us understand how it is used. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

Essential cookies are always on, because Northwind cannot work without them. You can switch the others on or off below at any time, and you can also clear cookies in your browser settings.
