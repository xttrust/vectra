Questions

Pricing, answered

Still unsure which plan fits? Tell us about your business and we will suggest one, honestly.

Is Solo really free?

Yes, for as long as you like. It covers one login, online booking and up to 20 invoices a month. Card payments cost the usual processing fee and nothing more.

What happens when my trial ends?

Nothing, unless you choose a plan. Your account drops to Solo and keeps all your data. We never charge a card you have not added.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time, from Settings. Moving up takes effect at once and we charge the difference for the rest of the month. Moving down applies from your next bill.

Do you charge per invoice or per booking?

No. Paid plans have no limits on invoices, quotes or bookings. Card payments are 1.4% plus 25 cent for European cards, paid straight to your bank.

Do you offer discounts?

Yearly billing saves two months. Charities and new businesses in their first year get 50% off Crew for twelve months: just ask.

How do I cancel?

From Settings, in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you can export everything first.

Try every feature free for 14 days.

No card needed. If it is not for you, your account simply moves to the free plan.
