Senior front-end developer

  • Tuam or remote (Ireland)
  • Full time
  • €70,000 to €85,000

The job

You will build the screens trades use every day: quotes, bookings and invoices, on phones with a weak signal and on old laptops in site offices. We care about speed, accessibility and plain words more than about the newest framework.

You will

  • Build and improve features with a designer and a back-end developer.
  • Keep the app fast and usable on cheap phones.
  • Spend a morning a month on customer calls.

You have

  • Five or more years building for the web, with strong HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Care for accessibility, and you have tested with a screen reader.
  • Experience with Laravel and Vue is a plus, not a must.

Apply in three short steps

About five minutes. We reply within a week.
