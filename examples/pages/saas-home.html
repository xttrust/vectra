Questions

Asked before you start

Something else? Ask us, a real person replies within a working day.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They book, accept quotes and pay from a link in their browser. Only you and your team sign in.

Can I move my existing customers over?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet or send us your old system’s export, and we will import it for free within two working days.

What does a card payment cost?

Card payments are 1.4% plus 25 cent for European cards, paid straight to your bank account. Bank transfers are free.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans run month to month, or yearly if you want the discount. Cancel any time and keep an export of everything.

Where is my data kept?

In data centres in Ireland and Germany, encrypted and backed up every night. We never sell or share it.
