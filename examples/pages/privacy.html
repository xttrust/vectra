This page shows how a legal page can be laid out. The wording is placeholder text for a made-up company and is not legal advice. Ask a solicitor to write yours.

Summary

  • We collect what we need to run Northwind, and no more.
  • We never sell your data or your customers' data.
  • Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland and Germany).
  • You can see, export or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

When you use Northwind we collect:

  • Account details: your name, email, phone number and business details.
  • Your customers' details: whatever you add, such as names, addresses and job notes. For this data you are the controller and we are your processor.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device type and approximate location, to keep the service secure and working well.

Why we use it

PurposeLegal basis
Running your account and the serviceContract
Billing and tax recordsLegal obligation
Keeping the service secureLegitimate interest
Product news by email (optional)Consent

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted suppliers to host data, take payments and send texts and emails. Each one is bound by a data processing agreement and keeps data in the EU or under approved safeguards. A full list is available on request.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you close it, we delete it within 30 days, except billing records, which the law requires us to keep for six years.

How we keep it safe

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up every night, and only a few named staff can reach production systems, always with two-step sign-in. We test our security every year with an outside firm.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email privacy@northwind.ie and we will reply within a month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland.

Contact

Our data protection lead can be reached at privacy@northwind.ie or by post at Unit 4, Dublin Road, Tuam, Co. Galway.
