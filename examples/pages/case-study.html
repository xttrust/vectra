Case study · Roofing · Galway

Harbour Roofing sends quotes in an hour, not a week.

Quote turnaround1 hourwas 6 days
Quotes accepted64%was 41%
Office hours saved9 a week

Before

Aoife Kelly runs the office for Harbour Roofing, a family firm with three crews. Quotes were written up at the kitchen table on Sunday nights, and by the time they arrived the customer had often gone with someone faster.

What changed

The crews now measure up and send the quote from the roof, with photos of the damage. Customers accept on their phone, and the job lands in the calendar with a deposit already paid.

"The first week, a customer accepted a quote before the lads had their ladders back on the van."
