Vectra states
Design direction

States and patterns · copy guide

Finally, the states in between.

Real products spend most of their screens loading, empty, failing, offline or waiting for someone to decide. Every state here comes with the words to use and the wiring that makes it work for everyone.

Each state has three parts of copy: what happened, why, and what the person can do next. Then a line ready to paste, and the accessibility wiring: which live region, where focus goes, and how much time people get.
