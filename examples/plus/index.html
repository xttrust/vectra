Context menu

Right-click the file panel, or focus it and press Shift+F10. The menu opens at the pointer and closes with Escape, returning focus to the panel. Without JS the browser's own menu shows.

Hover card

Rest the pointer on a linked name, or Tab to it: a preview opens after a moment and stays while you move onto it. Escape closes it. On touch a tap simply follows the link.
