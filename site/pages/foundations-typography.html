{{title}}

A fluid scale of six steps written with clamp(), so type grows with the viewport without breakpoints. Directions choose the fonts and the weight and tracking of headings.

The scale

--v-step-displayAa
--v-step-3Heading one
--v-step-2Heading two
--v-step-1Heading three
--v-step-0Body text
--v-step-minus-1Small text and captions

Type classes

.v-display for the biggest title on a page, .v-lead for the paragraph under it, .v-kicker for the small label above.

Fonts

--v-font-sans is Inter, self-hosted. --v-font-serif and --v-font-mono are system stacks. Directions point --v-font-display and --v-font-body at one of them: Editorial uses the serif for display type. The customizer's typeface preset (data-font) overrides the direction.

Prose

For CMS or markdown output with no classes, wrap it in .v-prose.
