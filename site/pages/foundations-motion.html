Enter and exit

Dialogs, drawers, popovers, menus and toasts animate in with @starting-style and out with transition-behavior: allow-discrete, all in CSS. Browsers without them snap open and closed.

Theme reveal

Vectra.theme.set() runs direction and mode changes inside a view transition where the browser has one, so the page cross-fades instead of flashing. Try the switch in the header.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, base.css switches transitions and animations off rather than shortening them, so a style change is never read back half-way. The status pulse and skeleton shimmer stop and the theme switch is instant. They are decoration: the words carry the meaning.
