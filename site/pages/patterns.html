Patterns

Components are the pieces; patterns are how they fit together on a real screen. These pages show them composed, including the states a product spends most of its time in: loading, empty, failed, offline.

{{galleries}}

States, in short

  • Loading. Skeletons for page loads, aria-busy on the button for an action, a determinate progress bar when the time is known. See Skeleton and Progress.
  • Empty. Say what will appear here and give one clear first action. See Empty state.
  • Errors. Field errors after the field is left, an error summary on submit, and a section that failed to load keeps the rest of the page working. See Error summary and Alert.
  • Connection and session. An offline banner that says what still works, and a session warning with enough time to react. See Connection banner and Session timeout.
  • Success. An inline "Saved" or a short toast, not both. See Save status and Toast.
