From 0.25rem to 4.5rem at the default density. --v-control-h (buttons, inputs) scales by half as much, so compact forms stay easy to tap.

Density

data-density="compact" on <html> sets --v-density: 0.85, "spacious" sets 1.15. Set the property yourself on any element to tighten one area, such as a data-heavy panel.
