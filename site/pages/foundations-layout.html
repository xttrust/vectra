{{title}}

Seven primitives, each with one job, tuned with a custom property rather than modifier classes. Most respond to the space they have, not to the viewport.

Stack

.v-stack spaces its children vertically by --v-gap.

Cluster

.v-cluster is an inline group that wraps: buttons, tags, toolbars.

Grid

.v-grid fits as many columns as it can, each at least --v-min wide (16rem by default). No breakpoints.

Sidebar

.v-sidebar puts a side panel (--v-side, 16rem) beside the content and stacks them once the content would drop below --v-content-min.

Switcher

.v-switcher keeps equal columns in a row and stacks them all at once below --v-switch (36rem).

Bento

.v-bento is a 12-column grid. Children span with .v-span-4 to .v-span-8 from 48rem and are full width below.

Container and section

.v-container centres a column with fluid gutters, up to --v-container-max (76rem; .v-container-narrow is 48rem, .v-container-wide 90rem). .v-section gives page sections their vertical rhythm, and .v-cq makes any element a size container for your own @container queries.
